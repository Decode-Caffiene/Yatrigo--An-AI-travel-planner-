# Yatrigo — AI Travel Planner

## The problem

Planning a trip today means juggling a dozen disconnected tools: a search
engine for "things to do in X," a spreadsheet for the day-by-day plan, separate
sites for flights, hotels, currency conversion, and visa rules, and a group
chat for coordinating with whoever you're traveling with. None of it talks to
each other, so the plan goes stale the moment one thing changes, and building
a realistic itinerary from scratch — one that actually accounts for your
budget, trip length, and interests — takes hours of manual research.

## What Yatrigo does about it

Yatrigo turns "I want to go to Japan for 10 days with a $2000 budget, I like
food and history" into a structured, day-by-day, city-by-city itinerary
automatically, using a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline instead
of a single unguided LLM prompt — so the plan is grounded in retrieved
destination context and validated before it's shown, rather than hallucinated.

Around that core, it consolidates the tools a traveler would otherwise have
open in ten tabs into one place:

- **Itinerary generation** — destination, budget, dates, and interests in;
  a validated day-by-day plan out.
- **Trip logistics in one app** — flights, hotels, weather, live currency
  conversion, maps, restaurants, local transit, visa requirements, and
  translation, all attached to the trip you're planning.
- **Planning with other people** — a community feed to get inspiration from
  other travelers, real-time messaging to coordinate with the people you're
  actually traveling with, and a quiz that recommends destinations if you
  don't know where to go yet.
- **Discovery** — trending destinations and a browsable destination guide for
  the "I don't have a destination yet" stage of planning.

## Who it's for

Independent travelers and small groups who want an AI-generated starting
itinerary they can trust  and don't want to re-research the same trip logistics across five
different sites once they have it.
